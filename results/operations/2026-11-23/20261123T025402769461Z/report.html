Evidence and limits

The 2023-2025 evaluation contains no drought targets under the frozen ERA5 definition, so drought recall cannot be estimated there. NASA's soil product disagrees on some cases. In the earlier historical check, a simple transition baseline outperformed the selected model on new onsets. This workflow preserves those findings and does not claim a validated best model.
